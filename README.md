# PrepScore

**PrepScore** is a practice analytics platform designed to help students track their SQL and DSA preparation through practice logs, consistency tracking, topic coverage, and readiness analytics.

The platform aims to answer a simple question:

> "Am I actually improving, or am I just solving random questions?"

---

## Features

### Current Development Goals

* User Registration and Login
* SQL & DSA Practice Tracking
* Topic and Subtopic Management
* Practice History
* Streak Tracking
* Readiness Score Calculation
* Progress Dashboard

## Problem Statement

Students often practice SQL and DSA consistently but lack a structured way to measure progress, identify weak areas, and evaluate overall preparation.

PrepScore provides a centralized platform that converts practice activity into meaningful preparation analytics.
